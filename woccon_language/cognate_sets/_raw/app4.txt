APPENDIX 4. CATAWBAN, BUT NOT IN CATAWBA (10) 
1. Bear *húka : W (Ourka) (error for (Ounka)) 'Bear-skin', PS *wihú'te 'bear'. (Proto-Siouan initial *|wi- is a pronominal element that marks non-human animates [Rankin 1998]). 
2. Canoe "wat: W (Watt) "A Canoe'; PS *wáti 'boat, canoe' 
3. Fox *hanatúkare W (Hanatockore) 'Fox-skin', PS *wihtóxka 'fox' 
(Proto-Siouan initial * wi- is a pronominal element that marks non- human animates [Rankin 1998]). 
4. Head *pápi : W (Poppe) ‘A Head', PS *pha 'head' 
5. Knife *w: W (Wee) 'Knife'; Lakota mi, mila ‘knife' 
6. Old Woman *yíku: W Yicau 'Old Woman', PS *ihkú 'grandmother' 
(The Woccon form appears to be the possessed form 'your old wom- an' with the second person singular prefix [yı-].) 
7. Panther *witá: W (Wattau) 'Panther-skin', PS *wihtá 'wildcat'. 8. Pine *húha W (Hooha) 'A Pine-tree', PS *hu 'tree, trunk, stalk'. 9. Raccoon *a hé: W (Auher) "Roccoon-skin', PS *wihé(-ka) *raccoon' 
(Proto-Siouan initial * wi- is a pronominal element that marks non- human animates [Rankin 1998]). 
10. Red *yárta: W (Yauta) 'Red', PS *rúti 'red'.
