APPENDIX 3. POSSIBLE COGNATES (6) 
1. Afraid: W (Reheshiwau) "Afraid': Cnqsyd·re. 'I am afraid' (root |-saya-- -sya-be afraid'). (The Woccon and Catawba words may share the same root; however, the composition of the Woccon word is unclear.) 
2. Bowl, Spoon: W (Cotsoo) 'Bowl', (Cotsau) 'Spoon': C yúkse 'spoon' 3. Box: W (Yopoonitsa) 'Box: C yap 'tree' plus another root (compare 
C yapkę 'box'). 
4. Fat: W (Yendare) 'Fat: C (dri 'new. (The semantic relationship of 
those words is uncertain.) 
5. Fish: W (Yacunne) 'Fish', Cyi 'fish' plus another root. (The Woc- 
con word may have named a particular kind of fish.) 
6. Little while ago: W (Yauka) 'A little while ago': C húkut 'now'
